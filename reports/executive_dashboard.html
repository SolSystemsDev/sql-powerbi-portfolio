Data quality and accounting conventions

Budget values reconcile to the source budget ledger. Actual expenses reconcile to the complete synthetic transaction ledger. Reporting joins on fiscal month, department and general-ledger account. All amounts are USD.

This is a portfolio simulation, not actual organizational financial information. Fiscal year is named by its ending September; e.g., FY2026 = 2025-10-01 through 2026-09-30. All figures are generated with seed 42.
